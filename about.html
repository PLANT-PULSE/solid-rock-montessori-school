WHO WE ARE

A prepared place to grow and belong.

Solid Rock Montessori School is located in Kissi, in the Komenda-Edina-Eguafo-Abirem (KEEA) District of Ghana's Central Region. We are shaping a nurturing early-years and primary environment where children can learn through purposeful activity, meaningful relationships and respect for the child.

Our Montessori-inspired approach supports child-led exploration, hands-on materials, concentration and increasing independence. Programme details and the school's history are to be confirmed by the school.

HOW WE TEACH

Guided by the child. Grounded in care.

Educators prepare an inviting environment and observe each child to offer timely guidance. Children work with concrete materials, learn through repetition and build concentration, language, early mathematics and practical life skills.

Group experiences, outdoor play and shared routines nurture communication, empathy and a sense of responsibility.
