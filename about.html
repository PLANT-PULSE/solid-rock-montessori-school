WHO WE ARE

A place to learn, belong and grow.

Morning Dew Academy is a school community in Bronyibima, KEEA District, Central Region, Ghana. We are committed to attentive teaching, good character and a learning environment that helps every child build confidence.

Our approach brings together experienced teachers, spacious classrooms, an ICT laboratory, outdoor activity and close partnership with parents.
